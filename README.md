## Selected Case Study: Ace Bank

**Organisation:** Ace Bank, a simulated mid-sized retail bank serving individual and small-business customers through branches, a mobile app and ATMs.

**Nature of the system:** A core banking database that manages customer accounts and the movement of money.

**Users of the database:** Customers, tellers, branch managers, compliance and audit officers, and the mobile app backend.

**Major data stored:** Customers, accounts, account types, branches, transactions, transfers, cards, loans, loan repayments and audit logs.

**Key operations supported:** Opening accounts, deposits, withdrawals, fund transfers, reversing failed transactions, generating account statements, auditing balances and flagging suspicious activity.

**DBMS used:** PostgreSQL
